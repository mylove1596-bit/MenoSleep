Support

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, write to support@menosleep.app.

Getting started

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The watch is optional. Without one you answer three questions in the morning and everything else works the same way.

Why do I have to answer the screening questions?

Because sleep restriction is not safe for everyone. If the screening finds something that makes the program inappropriate for you, we stop and point you to care instead of selling you a course. See the clinical disclaimer.

Why did my window change by only fifteen minutes?

On purpose. The window moves by fifteen minutes a week at most, and it only opens after two good weeks in a row. A single good week is not enough evidence. The app always shows the reason next to the number.

Apple Health

My sleep data is not showing up

Check three things: Apple Health has a sleep analysis source for your watch, the app is allowed to read Sleep Analysis in Settings, and the watch was worn and charged overnight. If the watch recorded less than four hours, the app asks you to fill in the night by hand instead.

Can I use the app without allowing HealthKit access?

Yes. Decline any permission you do not want. The app switches to manual mode and your program continues unchanged. The screen never stops you and never asks again until you open Settings and reconnect.

Does the app write anything into Apple Health?

No. MenoSleep only reads. It never writes, edits or deletes a sample, so it cannot interfere with your other health apps.

Your purchase

I bought it on my old phone

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Your entitlement is tied to your Apple ID, not to a device.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow Apple’s policy. Request a refund through the App Store, and we will make sure your access is adjusted accordingly.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase of $129 covering the six week course and twelve months of access. Nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged behind your back.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

Settings, then Delete everything, then confirm. That removes your profile, nights, sleep window, course progress, reviews and cached reports from this device and from your private iCloud database. The raw samples in Apple Health are not ours to delete, and your purchase record stays with Apple so you can restore it later.

Where can I see my data?

Settings, then Export my data. You get a CSV file you can open anywhere. You can also generate a PDF doctor report at any time.

Do you have my sleep data on a server?

No. Data lives on your device, and if you turn on iCloud sync it lives in your own private Apple CloudKit database. See the privacy policy.

Safety

The app stopped my sleep window

That is a safety rule, not a glitch. When you report daytime sleepiness, driving fatigue, worsening mood, pregnancy, or a seizure, the program stops adjusting your window and asks you to speak with a clinician. Do not work around it.

I am in crisis

Call your local emergency number. In the United States call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline; in the UK and Ireland call Samaritans on 116 123. MenoSleep cannot respond to an emergency.
