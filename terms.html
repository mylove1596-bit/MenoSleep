2. What MenoSleep is

MenoSleep is an educational program based on cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) principles, adapted for women going through perimenopause and menopause. It provides information, behavioural exercises, a sleep window schedule, and a summary report you may choose to share with a healthcare professional.

MenoSleep is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, a telehealth service, a prescription service, or a replacement for medical care. We do not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. We do not provide hormone therapy advice or any other treatment recommendation.
