How it works

  1. A short check-up. Twelve questions screen for things a sleep program should not touch, such as untreated sleep apnea, pregnancy, bipolar disorder, or severe daytime sleepiness. If we find one of those, we stop and help you find the right care instead of selling you a course.
  2. Your baseline. Seven nights, four are enough. With an Apple Watch your sleep data is read automatically; without one, three morning questions do the same job.
  3. Your first sleep window. A fixed wake time and a bedtime. The wake time is the anchor and does not move for six weeks.
  4. Six weeks, one change at a time. Each week we look at your sleep efficiency and adjust one thing.
  5. A maintenance plan and a doctor report. What to keep doing, and a page you can hand to your clinician.

Where the Apple Watch fits

The watch is optional, and it is used in three places only: it shows tonight's window before bed, it gives you a three minute breathing path if you wake at 3am without picking up your phone, and it confirms your night in the morning.

MenoSleep does not run overnight, does not ask for a workout session, and never watches for you while you sleep. Apple’s own sleep tracking records the night; our app reads it the next morning. If you do not wear a watch, nothing in the program is unavailable to you.

MenoSleep is not a medical device. It is an education program based on CBT-I principles. It does not diagnose insomnia, it does not prescribe, and it does not replace your doctor, your medication, or hormone therapy. If something does not feel safe, we stop the program and point you to a clinician.

Who it is for

Women between 35 and 65 with perimenopausal or menopausal sleep trouble: waking with hot flashes or night sweats, struggling to fall back asleep, or simply tired in the afternoon. If your symptoms are mild, we will not put you through sleep restriction at all; we will help you read your own data instead.

Your data

Your sleep data stays on your device. It is read from Apple Health, stored locally, and optionally synced to your own private iCloud database. There is no MenoSleep server holding your health data, and we do not run advertising or tracking. The doctor report only leaves your phone when you choose to share it.
