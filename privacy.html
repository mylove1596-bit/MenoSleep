Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to the MenoSleep iOS app and the MenoSleep app for Apple Watch.

MenoSleep is built so that your health data stays on your device. We do not operate a server that holds your sleep data, we do not sell or share it with advertisers, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

The short version. We read sleep and health data from Apple Health only if you allow it. We calculate your sleep metrics on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, your calculated records are stored in your own private Apple CloudKit database. We only ever see data that you explicitly share, such as a doctor report you export.

1. Who we are

MenoSleep (“we”, “us”) is a sleep education program for women going through perimenopause and menopause. It is based on CBT-I principles and is not a medical device. This policy explains what the app does with data. For questions, contact us at privacy@menosleep.app.

2. Data we read from Apple Health

When you connect Apple Health, the app asks for each type separately and tells you why. You can decline any of them, and the program still works, using a short morning diary instead.

Data typeWhy we read itWhere it goesHow long we keep it
Sleep analysis (in bed, asleep, core, deep, REM, awake) To calculate time in bed, total sleep time, time to fall asleep, awake time after falling asleep, and your number of night awakenings Read on your device. MenoSleep reads these types only. It never writes, edits or deletes anything in Apple Health, so it cannot affect your other health apps or your Apple Health history.

4. Data the app calculates on your device

From the data above, MenoSleep calculates sleep efficiency, total sleep time, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, night awakening counts, your weekly sleep window, and your progress through the program. These are called “night summaries” in the table above. They are stored in the app’s local database, and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your private CloudKit database.

5. Where data is stored

  • On your device. Your profile, night summaries, sleep window, course progress and reviews are stored locally in the app.
  • In your private iCloud database. If you enable sync in Settings, the same records are stored in your own CloudKit private database. Apple holds that data on your behalf. We cannot see it, and we cannot query it.
  • Not in our servers. MenoSleep has no backend that stores your health data. We do not run a database of users, and we do not use third-party analytics that receive health values.
  • Doctor reports. The PDF is generated locally with PDFKit and written to a temporary location on your device. It leaves the device only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

6. Purchases

MenoSleep is sold as a single non-consumable in-app purchase through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; we receive an anonymous transaction identifier so we can confirm your entitlement. We never see or store your card details. Your purchase record is managed by Apple, so deleting your MenoSleep data does not affect your ability to restore the purchase on a new device. Refunds follow Apple’s refund policy.

7. Diagnostics and usage measurement

We record that a screen was opened or an action was completed, for example screener_completed, lesson_finished, paywall_viewed, with coarse properties such as week=3 or source=watch. These events never contain your sleep duration, sleep efficiency, heart rate, temperature or any other health value. The operating system may generate a crash log if the app stops unexpectedly; those logs do not contain your health data.

8. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not use the advertising identifier.
  • We do not use your health data to make a diagnosis, and we do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not show your sleep data to anyone without your action.

9. Sharing, in practice

  • Apple. Apple processes health data on your behalf, stores it in HealthKit and iCloud, and processes your payment.
  • Your doctor report. Only after you generate it and share it through the system share sheet. Once shared, that file is outside the app, and deleting your data in MenoSleep does not remove the copy you already sent.
  • Legal requests. We may be required to disclose information if we receive a valid legal process. Because we do not operate a server holding your health data, in practice we usually hold nothing to disclose beyond basic app and purchase metadata.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Your MenoSleep records are kept until you delete them.
  • Delete everything in Settings removes your profile, night summaries, sleep windows, course progress, reviews and cached reports from this device and from your private iCloud database. CloudKit deletions complete within 24 hours.
  • Raw samples in Apple Health are not ours. They stay in HealthKit unless you delete them there. This is stated in the confirmation dialog before deletion, and again in Settings.
  • Your purchase record stays with Apple, so you can restore your purchase later.

11. Getting your data

Settings lets you export your records as a CSV file at any time, and generate a PDF doctor report. Both are produced on your device and shared through the system share sheet. MenoSleep never uploads an export automatically.

12. Security

Health data is stored in the app’s private container and, if you enable sync, in your private CloudKit database, encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. We do not add our own server layer, which means there is no additional place where your data could leak from.

13. Children

MenoSleep is intended for adults, and specifically for adults 35 to 65 going through perimenopause or menopause. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

14. International transfers

Data is stored by Apple in the region where your Apple ID or iCloud account is located. If you contact support, we may see information you choose to include in your message.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is handled, we will update the version number shown above, publish the new text here, and ask you to review and agree again inside the app. The version you agreed to is stored in your profile.

16. Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to exercise any of your rights, write to privacy@menosleep.app. General help is on the support page.
