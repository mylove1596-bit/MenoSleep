Clinical Disclaimer

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. In the UK and Ireland, call 116 123 (Samaritans). What MenoSleep is

MenoSleep is a self-guided education program built on cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) principles, adapted for the sleep problems that accompany perimenopause and menopause. CBT-I is the approach with randomised controlled trial evidence for insomnia, and it is also used in menopause-related sleep complaints.

The program teaches behavioural techniques: a fixed wake time, stimulus control, a time-in-bed window that adjusts to your data, cognitive strategies for night-time worry, and practical changes for hot flashes and night sweats.

What MenoSleep is not

  • It does not diagnose insomnia, sleep apnea, or any other condition.
  • It does not treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
  • It does not prescribe medication, and it does not give advice about hormone therapy, antidepressants, or any other treatment.

  • It is not a substitute for emergency or crisis care.

Improvements described in the app, such as an average increase in sleep efficiency across program participants, are goals for the program as a whole. They are not promises about what will happen to any individual, and many people will need medical care alongside or instead of this program.

Why the app screens you first

Sleep restriction is not safe for everyone. It can trigger mania in people with bipolar disorder, lower the seizure threshold in people with epilepsy, and mask the severity of untreated sleep apnea. The app therefore asks twelve questions before it will let you start.

If any of the following applies to you, the app will stop the program and suggest you speak with a clinician first:

  • Thoughts of harming yourself, or a PHQ-9 score of 15 or above
  • Bipolar disorder, a history of mania, or a psychotic disorder
  • Epilepsy, or a seizure history that is not fully controlled
  • Pregnancy, or trying to become pregnant
  • Untreated sleep apnea, or heavy snoring with choking or gasping
  • Alcohol or sedative dependence, or withdrawal
  • Severe daytime sleepiness (an Epworth-style score above 15), including dozing while driving

Rotating or night shift work, and frequent travel across time zones, also mean a fixed sleep window does not fit your life. In that case you will be placed on a waitlist for a version built around shifting schedules.

Safety rules built into the program

The app pauses sleep restriction immediately, and asks you to speak with a clinician, if any of the following is reported: a very high daytime sleepiness score, meaningful impairment during the day, dozing off while driving, worsening depression, signs of mania or hypomania, a seizure, pregnancy, or seven consecutive nights of less than five hours of sleep. If your window has been at the shortest allowed length for two weeks without improvement, the program stops the restriction and refers you to a doctor.

Stopping the restriction is not a failure. It is the program working as intended.

How to read your data

Heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate and wrist temperature appear in the app as background trends only. A wrist temperature difference does not mean the app detected a hot flash, and an unusual respiratory rate does not mean the app diagnosed sleep apnea. These values are shown so that you and your clinician have more context, not so that the app can draw a conclusion about your health.

When to see a doctor

Please speak with a healthcare professional if:

  • Your sleep problem is getting worse rather than better during the program
  • You feel drowsy while driving or operating machinery
  • You notice heavy snoring with choking, or witnessed breathing pauses during sleep
  • Your mood is dropping, or you have thoughts of harming yourself
  • You develop hot flashes that are severe, or urinary symptoms that are new
  • You are pregnant, or planning pregnancy
  • You want to discuss hormone therapy, medication, or screening tests such as a sleep study

The doctor report

The PDF report exists to support a conversation with your clinician. It shows your baseline against your current week: sleep efficiency, total sleep time, time to fall asleep, awake time after falling asleep, night awakenings, hot flash frequency, how many nights you actually recorded, how your sleep window moved, and anything we flagged for attention. Heart rate and temperature trends are excluded by default.

This report supports a conversation with your clinician. It is not a diagnosis.

Clinical oversight

The screening questions, safety rules, six week course scripts, sleep window algorithm, report template and disclaimers are reviewed by an independent clinical adviser before release. The program is also piloted with a small number of participants under clinical supervision before wider release.

Contact

If something in the app contradicts advice you have been given by your clinician, stop using it and contact us at clinical@menosleep.app so we can review it.
